EYEWAZ Urdu Voice

A natural, fully offline Urdu voice for NVDA, powered by EYEWAZ and trained on real WAJD AI voices. NVDA reads Urdu in a clear human voice with no internet connection and no per character cost.

Select the voice

  1. Open NVDA menu, Preferences, Settings, Speech.
  2. Under Synthesizer, choose EYEWAZ Urdu Voice.
  3. Under Voice, choose EYEWAZ Urdu Female or EYEWAZ Urdu Male.
  4. Set Rate and Volume to taste. NVDA now reads in Urdu.

Privacy

All speech is generated on your own computer. The add-on makes no network connections and sends no text anywhere.
