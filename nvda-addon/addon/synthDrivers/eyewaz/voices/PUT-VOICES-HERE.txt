Put your trained EYEWAZ Piper voices in THIS folder.

Each voice is two files with matching names, for example:
  eyewaz-urdu-female.onnx
  eyewaz-urdu-female.onnx.json
  eyewaz-urdu-male.onnx
  eyewaz-urdu-male.onnx.json

These are the files produced by the training pipeline (see ../../../../tts-train/
train_colab.md, the export to ONNX step). Drop them in and the driver lists each
one as a voice in NVDA, Speech settings (for example "EYEWAZ Urdu Female").

The file name becomes the voice name: eyewaz-urdu-female -> EYEWAZ Urdu Female.
