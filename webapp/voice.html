An Urdu voice for your Windows screen reader

A free, natural Urdu voice for NVDA, JAWS and Narrator. It works fully offline, so nothing you read ever leaves your computer.

What you get

  • Clear Urdu speech in a female or a male voice.
  • Word tracking, so your screen reader can follow and highlight each word.
  • Works in NVDA, JAWS, Narrator, Microsoft Word Read Aloud, and any program that uses Windows speech.
  • No internet needed and no per word cost, ever.

Your privacy: the voice runs entirely on your own computer. The text you read is spoken on the device and is never sent to any server or company, including us.

Install it

Choose your screen reader. Both are free.

NVDA

  1. In NVDA, press NVDA then N, and choose Tools, then Add-on Store.
  2. Search for EYEWAZ Urdu and choose Install. Let NVDA restart when asked.
  3. Open Preferences > Settings > Speech, choose Change, and pick EYEWAZ Urdu. Then choose the female or male voice.

Or download the add-on .nvda-addon file, for NVDA 2021.1 and newer

JAWS, Narrator or Word

  1. Download the installer below and run it. Approve the Windows prompt that asks to allow changes.
  2. Finish the short setup. The voice is ready straight away, with nothing else to configure.
  3. In JAWS, open Options > Voices > Voice Adjustment and pick EYEWAZ Urdu (Female) or EYEWAZ Urdu (Male). In Narrator, open Narrator settings > Narrator's voice.

Download the Windows installer for 64 bit Windows 10 and 11

Removing the voice

For NVDA, remove the add-on in the Add-on Store. For the Windows voice, open Settings > Apps > Installed apps, find EYEWAZ Urdu Voice, and choose Uninstall.
