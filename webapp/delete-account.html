Delete your account

You can delete your EYEWAZ account and all of your data at any time. You do not need the app installed to request deletion.

From inside the app

Sign in, open Account, then choose Delete account and confirm. Your account is removed straight away.

Open the app

By email

Email us from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm and complete the deletion for you.

privacy@eyewaz.com

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes your profile (name, email, phone), your saved library and recordings, your reminders, and your preferences from our servers within 30 days. Voice bank contributors may also withdraw consent at any time and we delete the contributed recordings and any voice built from them. Payment records held by our payment providers are kept only as long as the law requires. For the full detail, read our privacy policy.
